Where do I find my run's file?

Export the activity you ran, not a route or course: a route has no times in it, so it can't be timed. Most apps only export from their website, not the phone app; the website works in a phone's browser too.

Garmin
On connect.garmin.com, open the activity, click the gear icon at the top right and choose Export to GPX. (Export Original gives a .zip: unzip it and upload the .fit inside.)
Strava
On strava.com, open the activity, click the ⋯ button on the left and choose Export GPX. It has to be your own activity. On a phone, open strava.com in the browser and ask for the desktop site.
COROS
In the COROS app, open the activity, tap the share or ⋯ button and choose Export data, then GPX or FIT.
Polar
On flow.polar.com, open the training session and choose Export session, then GPX.
Suunto
In the Suunto app, open the workout, tap ⋯ and choose to export it as GPX.
Apple Watch
Apple Health can't export one run on its own. Let it sync to Strava and export from there, or use an app such as HealthFit or RunGap to export it.
Anything else
Look for Export, Download or Share on the activity. If your app syncs to Strava or Garmin, export from there.

Uploading it: the file usually lands in your Downloads. Tap Choose file above; on an iPhone pick Choose File → Browse (the Files app) and look in Downloads, on Android pick Files or Downloads. Then press Get my time.

If it doesn't work: a .zip needs unzipping first (a .gz is fine as it is). If it says the run has no times, you exported a route; export the activity instead. If it says you didn't reach the start or finish, check the map: your run has to pass the green dot and follow the red line to the finish.
