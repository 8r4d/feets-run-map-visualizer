Guests and sharing

There are two ways to let someone see your feets map without being able to change anything:

  • A view link: anyone with the link can open it, with no account or login. It shows your saved routes only.
  • A view-only account: a guest account the admin invites someone to make. They log in and see your whole map: activities, streets and routes.

View links

A view link is the easy way to share routes, with a club or a friend you're meeting up with. To make one, open Account and choose Share view link. Give the link a name, like who it's for. It works for 7 days, or until you turn it off. (The admin can choose how many days their own links last, or keep one working until they turn it off.)

Someone opening the link sees:

  • your saved routes, on a plain map, with the newest one (or the one the link points to) already open
  • each route's distance and elevation, a GPX button to put it on their watch, and Share to pass that route on
  • when the link expires, if it does, under your name at the top

They don't see your activities, stats, goals, trophies or street coverage, and they can't change anything.

Sharing one route

In Planning, Link on a saved route makes a link that opens that route. The route gets its own link, named "Route: route name" in your list under Account, which works for 7 days.

The admin is asked how many days it should work instead:

  • A number of days: the route gets its own link, as above.
  • Blank: it uses your newest view link that doesn't expire, or offers to make one.

A route link is a view link that opens on that route. Anyone with it can also look at your other saved routes, so delete any you'd rather keep to yourself.

When links expire

  • A link lasts 7 days. The admin can choose from 1 to 365 days, or until they turn it off, when they make it.
  • The time counts from when the link was made. The people you send it to see the exact date and time it expires, in their own time zone.
  • Once it expires, the link opens the login screen with a note that it has been turned off or has expired. Nothing of yours is shown.
  • Under Account, each link shows "(until …)" or "(expired)". Turn off ends a link straight away, whether or not it has expired.
  • A link's expiry can't be changed. To give someone more time, make a new link and send them that.

View-only accounts

For someone who wants to follow along more closely, such as a partner or a coach, the admin can choose Invite viewer under Account. This makes an invite link that works once, within 7 days. The person opening it picks their own username and password.

A view-only account sees the admin's map as the admin does: activities, the heatmap, street coverage, stats, goals, trophies and saved routes. They can't add, change, download or delete anything. A few things are still theirs to use, and they only change the view in their own browser:

  • showing and hiding tracks, filters, track colours and ◎
  • the map mode and fade
  • drawing a route in Planning and downloading it as GPX (they can't save it)

A view-only account doesn't expire the way a view link does.

Virtual races

A virtual race lets a group race one of your routes, each running it when they like within a few days, and see who was fastest. In Planning, choose Race on a saved route, give the race a name and its first and last days, and send the link you're given. Anyone with it can open the race's page, with no account: it shows the course on a map, the leaderboard, and a form to enter.

To enter, someone runs the course and uploads the run (GPX, FIT or TCX). feets times it from the course's start to its finish, wherever they started their watch, and puts the time on the leaderboard. For a run to count it has to:

  • pass within about 30 m of the start, then reach the finish after about the course's length
  • follow the course most of the way (within about 40 m), covering at least 90% of it, so a short cut doesn't count
  • start and finish between the race's first and last days

Runs can be uploaded for 2 days after the last day, for anyone who ran on it. Each person gets their best time: uploading a faster run replaces it, and a slower one doesn't. That works per browser, so an entry made on a phone and another on a laptop show as two times. Only each runner's name and time are kept, never their run.

Your races are listed under Account, where you can copy a race's link, change its name or days, end it now or delete it. Opening a race's page while you're logged in lets you take times off its leaderboard. A copy of the route is made when the race is, so changing the route afterwards doesn't change the race.

Opening a link when you have an account

If you're logged in to feets and open someone's view link, you see their shared routes, just as a guest would. Choose Log in at the top to leave the link and go back to your own map.
