Guests and sharing

There are two ways to let someone see your feets map without being able to change anything:

  • A view link: anyone with the link can open it, with no account or login. It shows your saved routes only.
  • A view-only account: a guest account the admin invites someone to make. They log in and see your whole map: runs, streets and routes.

View links

A view link is the easy way to share routes, with a running club or a friend you're meeting for a run. To make one, open Account and choose Share view link. Give the link a name, like who it's for, and say how many days it should work. Leave the days blank to keep it working until you turn it off.

Someone opening the link sees:

  • your saved routes, on a plain map, with the newest one (or the one the link points to) already open
  • each route's distance and elevation, a GPX button to put it on their watch, and Share to pass that route on
  • when the link expires, if it does, under your name at the top

They don't see your runs, stats, goals, trophies or street coverage, and they can't change anything.

Sharing one route

In Plan mode, Link on a saved route makes a link that opens that route. It asks how many days the link should work:

  • A number of days: the route gets its own link, named "Route: route name" in your list under Account.
  • Blank: it uses your newest view link that doesn't expire, or offers to make one.

A route link is a view link that opens on that route. Anyone with it can also look at your other saved routes, so delete any you'd rather keep to yourself.

When links expire

  • A link can last from 1 to 365 days, or until you turn it off. You choose when you make it.
  • The time counts from when the link was made. The people you send it to see the exact date and time it expires, in their own time zone.
  • Once it expires, the link opens the login screen with a note that it has been turned off or has expired. Nothing of yours is shown.
  • Under Account, each link shows "(until …)" or "(expired)". Turn off ends a link straight away, whether or not it has expired.
  • A link's expiry can't be changed. To give someone more time, make a new link and send them that.

View-only accounts

For someone who wants to follow along more closely, such as a partner or a coach, the admin can choose Invite viewer under Account. This makes an invite link that works once, within 7 days. The person opening it picks their own username and password.

A view-only account sees the admin's map as the admin does: runs, the heatmap, street coverage, stats, goals, trophies and saved routes. They can't add, change, download or delete anything. A few things are still theirs to use, and they only change the view in their own browser:

  • showing and hiding tracks, filters, track colours and ◎
  • the map mode and fade
  • drawing a route in Plan mode and downloading it as GPX (they can't save it)

A view-only account doesn't expire the way a view link does.

Opening a link when you have an account

If you're logged in to feets and open someone's view link, you see their shared routes, just as a guest would. Choose Log in at the top to leave the link and go back to your own map.
